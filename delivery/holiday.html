How holiday camps work

Holiday programmes run during each school break period across multiple Canterbury venues. Children are grouped by age into the appropriate development pathway — Little Movers, Fundamentals, or Performance Pathway — and experience a full day of structured sport, games, and challenges.

Camps follow the same achievement system as our term-time programmes, so children can continue earning badges during the holidays.

Frequently asked questions

What ages are holiday camps for?

Holiday camps cater for children aged 2–12, grouped into Little Movers (2–4), Fundamentals (5–8), and Performance Pathway (9–12).

Where are camps held?

Camps run at various venues across Canterbury. Specific locations are confirmed before each school break period.

Are there full-day and half-day options?

Yes. Both options are typically available, depending on the venue and school break period.
