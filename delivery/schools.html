How it works

Proteus coaches deliver structured sport and physical development sessions within the school day, during aftercare, or as special events. All sessions follow the same developmental framework used across our programmes — Little Movers for the youngest children, Fundamentals for primary-age, and Performance Pathway for older students.

Schools can choose from regular weekly sessions, term programmes, or one-off events like sports days and inter-school competitions.

Frequently asked questions

Which age groups do you work with in schools?

We deliver programmes for all age groups from 2 to 12, matched to the appropriate development pathway. Sessions are adapted for the school context.

How do schools get started?

Contact us to discuss your school's needs. We'll work with you to design a programme that fits your timetable, facilities, and student needs.
