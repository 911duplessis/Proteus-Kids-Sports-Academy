Common questions

Which programme is right for my child?

Children are placed by age: 2–4 in Little Movers, 5–8 in Fundamentals, 9–12 in Performance Pathway. If you're unsure, email us and we'll help you find the right fit.

Can my child do a trial session?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a trial session at a venue near you. Trial availability depends on the programme and current capacity.

Where are sessions held?

Sessions run at schools, community venues, and sports grounds across Canterbury. Specific locations vary by programme and term.

Is Proteus Kids Sports Academy part of Proteus Sports Group?

Yes. Proteus Kids Sports Academy is the children's development arm of Proteus Sports Group, focused specifically on age-based physical development for 2–12 year olds.
