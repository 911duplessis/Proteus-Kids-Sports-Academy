# PROTEUS Brand Architecture v1.0

**Status:** Approved Foundation Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the official brand architecture for Proteus and supersedes previous ecosystem diagrams. It establishes a single naming system for all future website, marketing, programme, certificate, mascot, and digital assets.

---

# 1. Master Brand

**Proteus Sports Group**

The parent organisation responsible for governance, partnerships, schools, community programmes, coach development, and future business expansion.

The parent brand is primarily business-facing.

---

# 2. Consumer Brand

**Proteus Kids Sports Academy**

This remains the official consumer-facing brand.

No rename will occur.

This preserves continuity with:

* Existing website
* GitHub repository
* Existing documentation
* SEO assets
* Marketing collateral
* Certificates
* Social media
* Domain structure

The previous suggestion of **Proteus Kids Sports™** is withdrawn to avoid unnecessary disruption.

---

# 3. Programme Structure

Proteus Kids Sports Academy contains multiple programmes rather than being an age category itself.

### Early Years

**Little Movers**

* Ages 2–4
* Fundamental movement
* Balance
* Coordination
* Confidence through play

### Foundation

**Fundamentals**

* Ages 5–8
* Core athletic skills
* Multi-sport exposure
* Speed
* Agility
* Teamwork

This replaces the proposed "Active Kids" name because "Fundamentals" already exists in the live programme structure.

### Academy

**Sports Academy**

* Ages 9–12
* Advanced development
* Game understanding
* Leadership
* Performance preparation

This is a programme within Proteus Kids Sports Academy—not the brand itself.

---

# 4. Existing Programmes

Current programmes remain unchanged.

They are organised beneath the Academy brand rather than renamed.

## Schools' Sports

School delivery programme.

## Holiday Programmes

Umbrella category containing individual branded camps.

Examples include:

* Papanui Redwood Camp
* Prebbleton FC Camp
* Real Madrid Foundation Camp
* Westland United Camp

These camps retain their established names for recognition and SEO while sitting under the Holiday Programmes category.

## Community Organisation

Community engagement and outreach.

---

# 5. Progression System

Two complementary systems operate together.

## A. Development Levels

These represent where a child is in their journey.

Little Movers → Fundamentals → Sports Academy

A child progresses through these over several years.

## B. Achievement Badges

These measure competencies rather than age.

Current badge set: Explorer, Focus, Agility, Teamwork, Resilience, Champion

Children earn badges while remaining within their current development level.

Levels define progression. Badges define achievement. The two systems do not compete.

---

# 6. Certificates

Every certificate contains: Programme Level, Achievement Badges earned, Coach comments, Date, Next development milestone.

---

# 7. Parent Portal

Each child profile displays: Current Programme → Skills achieved → Badge collection → Coach feedback → Progress history → Next milestone.

---

# 8. Brand Hierarchy

Proteus Sports Group → Proteus Kids Sports Academy → Little Movers / Fundamentals / Sports Academy / Schools' Sports / Holiday Programmes / Community Organisation

---

# 9. Future Expansion

Additional divisions can be introduced without affecting the Academy brand: Proteus Coach Academy, Proteus Performance, Proteus Events, Proteus Community, Proteus Education.

---

## Final Decision

* Parent brand remains **Proteus Sports Group**.
* Consumer brand remains **Proteus Kids Sports Academy**.
* Existing live programme names are preserved where they already have recognition.
* Development Levels and Achievement Badges operate together as complementary systems.
* This document becomes the authoritative reference for Phase 2 (brand assets), Phase 3 (programme materials), website updates, and future product development.
