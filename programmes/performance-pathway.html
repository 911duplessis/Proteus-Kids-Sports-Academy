How Performance Pathway works

Performance Pathway builds on the multi-sport foundation established in Fundamentals. Children begin to develop sport-specific technical skills while also learning tactical awareness, decision-making under pressure, and how to lead and support teammates.

Sessions are more structured than earlier pathways and introduce elements of competitive sport — but always within the context of continued development. The goal is to prepare children for club sport, representative pathways, and lifelong physical activity.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Performance Pathway for?

Performance Pathway is designed for children aged 9 to 12. Younger children (5–8) should be in Fundamentals; children under 5 start with Little Movers.

Does my child need to have done Fundamentals first?

No. Children join Performance Pathway based on age. However, children who have progressed through Fundamentals will have a broader movement foundation to build on.

Is this competitive sport?

Performance Pathway introduces competitive elements within a developmental framework. The focus remains on skill development, game understanding, and character — not just winning.
