How Little Movers works

Little Movers sessions are structured around purposeful play — activities designed to develop fundamental movement patterns in a fun, supportive environment. Children learn to run, jump, throw, catch, balance, and move confidently through space.

Sessions are led by trained Proteus coaches who understand early childhood development. Every activity is age-appropriate and designed to build physical literacy from the ground up.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Little Movers for?

Little Movers is designed for children aged 2 to 4. Children aged 5 and up progress to Fundamentals.

Does a parent need to stay?

For the youngest children (2–3), a parent or caregiver stays with the child during sessions. Details vary by venue and session format.

What should my child wear?

Comfortable clothing that allows free movement, and appropriate footwear for the venue (indoor or outdoor). No special equipment is needed.
