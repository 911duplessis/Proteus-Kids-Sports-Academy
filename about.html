About Us

Proteus Kids Sports Academy

A Proteus Sports Group programme delivering age-based physical development for children aged 2–12 across Canterbury, New Zealand.

We develop the whole child — not just the athlete. Through three progressive pathways, children build movement confidence, physical literacy, and life skills in an environment that prioritises development over results.

Our Philosophy

Movement Before Competition

We believe every child deserves to experience the joy of movement before they experience the pressure of competition. Our development pathways — Little Movers, Fundamentals, and Performance Pathway — are designed around what children need at each stage of their physical development.

Our Approach

What makes us different.

🧠
Development-Led
Programmes are structured around developmental stages, not competitive calendars. Children progress when they're ready.
🏃
Multi-Sport Foundation
Broad movement literacy across multiple sports before specialisation. Better athletes, fewer injuries, more enjoyment.
⭐
Achievement System
Six competency-based badges recognise real skills — not just participation. Children see their own progress.
What We Do

Three pathways. One journey.

AGES 2–4
Little Movers
Balance, coordination, and motor skills built through purposeful play.
 AGES 5–8
Fundamentals
Multi-sport exposure across six sports — core athletic skills, speed, agility, and teamwork.
 AGES 9–12
Performance Pathway
Sport-specific development, game understanding, leadership, and performance preparation.
