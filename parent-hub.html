Frequently asked questions

How does the badge system work?

Children earn competency-based Achievement Badges (Explorer, Focus, Agility, Teamwork, Resilience, Champion) by demonstrating specific skills during sessions. Badges are earned through demonstrated ability, not time served.

How does my child move between pathways?

Children progress from Little Movers to Fundamentals to Performance Pathway based on age and developmental readiness. Badge progress carries across pathways.

Can my child start at any level?

Children are placed in pathways by age: 2–4 in Little Movers, 5–8 in Fundamentals, 9–12 in Performance Pathway. Within each pathway, all children start earning badges from Explorer regardless of prior experience.
